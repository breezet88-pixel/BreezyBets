# Ready-to-send enquiry email to a provincial gambling board

Send this to the **Licensing Department** of the board you choose. You have not decided the province yet, so you can send it to either (or both):

- **Gauteng Gambling Board** — your home province. Find the current licensing contact at **ggb.org.za**.
- **Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board** — often cited as the most accessible for online-only bookmakers. Contact at **wcgrb.co.za**.

Fill the two blanks, then send. It is an enquiry, so it costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

---

**Subject:** Enquiry — new online bookmaker licence application

Good day

I am planning to establish an online fixed-odds sports and racing bookmaker, to be operated by a South African company I am registering, **Apex Bets (Pty) Ltd**. It will be online only, in English, offering betting on sport and racing — no casino-style games. I am the sole owner (100% black-owned) and am based in Gauteng.

Before I prepare an application, could you please confirm:

1. Is the Board currently accepting new online bookmaker licence applications?
2. Which application forms and supporting documents are required for a new online-only bookmaker, and what are the current fees and any refundable deposit?
3. Is a physical office or licensed premises required for an online-only operation, and if so, what must it consist of? May a registered company address be used initially?
4. Must the applicant or the business be based in your province, or may a company based in another province (Gauteng) hold your licence for online operation?
5. Which individuals (owner, director, key staff) require personal probity clearance or key-employee licences, and what does that involve?
6. What certification must the betting platform hold (for example SANS 1718) before the licence can be used?
7. What is the current typical processing time from a complete application to a decision?

Thank you for your assistance. I would be grateful for any applicant guide or checklist you can share.

Kind regards
Breeze Thulani Kobola
[YOUR EMAIL] · [YOUR CELL NUMBER]

---

*Tip: send it, then wait for their reply before spending anything on lawyers or platforms. Their answer to questions 1, 3 and 4 decides whether the Western Cape or Gauteng route is open to you.*
