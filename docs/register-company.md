# Registering Apex Bets (Pty) Ltd — step by step

**Can Claude do this for you? No — and no one honest can do it without you.** Registering a company legally requires *your* identity verification, *your* consent as director, and *your* payment. You do it yourself on the government's own site in about 20 minutes. It is cheap and genuinely DIY — you do not need a lawyer or an agent for this part.

> **Do not send your ID number to Claude or to anyone by email/chat.** You type it only into the government website below.

## Use BizPortal (the government's one-stop site)

**bizportal.gov.za** is run by the CIPC (the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission). It registers the company, reserves the name, gives you a B-BBEE affidavit, registers you for income tax, and can even start a business bank account application — in one flow.

### What to have ready
- Your **South African ID number** and your name exactly as on your ID: **Breeze Thulani Kobola**.
- An **email address** and **cell number** (used for verification).
- **Up to 4 proposed company names**, in order of preference (the first free one is used). Suggestions: `Apex Bets`, `Apex Bets SA`, `Apex Betting`, `Apex Wagers`. Have alternatives because a name may already be taken.
- Your **registered address** — your Gauteng home address (full street address and postal code) is fine until you have business premises.
- A **card or EFT** to pay the fee.

### The steps
1. Go to **bizportal.gov.za** and create a customer profile with your ID, email and cell. You verify your identity here.
2. Choose **Register a Company** → **Private Company (Pty) Ltd**.
3. Enter your **proposed names** (up to 4).
4. Enter **director and shareholder details**: you, Breeze Thulani Kobola, as sole director and 100% shareholder.
5. Enter the **registered address** (your Gauteng address).
6. **Pay** (about **R175**, including one name reservation).
7. You receive the **registration certificate (CoR14.3)** and company **registration number**, usually within a day or two. Keep the MOI (founding document) it gives you.
8. While there, also request: the **B-BBEE affidavit** (free — proves 100% black ownership, likely Level 1), and note your company is auto-registered for **income tax** with SARS.

### After registration
- **Bank account:** with the registration number you can open a business bank account (FNB, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank, Capitec Business, TymeBank, Bank Zero). Declare the intended business honestly; gambling is treated as high-risk, so a normal account now and the gambling-specific conversation later, once licensed.
- **Keep safe:** registration number, MOI, B-BBEE affidavit, tax number. The licence application needs all of them.

**Important:** registering the company does **not** let you take real bets. It is only the legal shell. Real betting needs the licence, which is a separate, much larger step (`docs/licence.md`).
