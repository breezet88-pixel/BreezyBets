## The one line
**Apex Bets is a built, mobile-first South African sportsbook that wins on the two things punters complain about most — getting paid fast, and being treated straight — in a R52-billion betting market growing ~44% a year.**

## The opportunity
- South African **betting revenue was about R52 billion in 2024/25 and grew roughly 44%** year on year; online is about 60% of all gambling revenue (National Gambling Board figures — see `docs/competitive-analysis.md`).
- The market is a **duopoly** (Betway + Hollywoodbets hold most of the traffic) but newcomers like EasyBet and 10bet have grown fast, so a focused challenger can take share.
- Bettors are **mobile, low-stake, and price-conscious on data.**

## The problem we attack
Public complaints and industry research point to the same gaps at the big operators: **withdrawals that are slow or accounts locked when you try to cash out**, cluttered apps, heavy mobile-data use, and responsible-gambling tools that are hidden. (Evidence and sources in `docs/competitive-analysis.md`.)

## The product — already built and demonstrable
A working platform exists today (play-money demo), not a slide deck:
- **A bet in three taps**, English, sport and racing only (no illegal casino games).
- **Fast, tracked withdrawals:** small amounts auto-approved day or night; a live tracker shows every step to "paid"; measured payout times published to customers.
- **Trust and safety built in, not bolted on:** a double-entry, append-only wallet ledger that keeps customer money separate and auditable; deposit limits chosen at sign-up; self-imposed breaks; a plain "this month you're up/down R…" statement.
- **Light on data:** measured at roughly **0.3 MB per hour** watching a live match — cheap to use and cheaper to zero-rate with networks.
- **Tested:** an automated test suite covers accounts, the wallet, betting and withdrawals.

## Why now
Market growth is strong, challengers are proving newcomers can grow, and the compliance bar is rising — which favours an operator that is **compliant by design from day one** rather than retrofitting it.

## Business model
Revenue is **gross gaming revenue** — stakes minus winnings, from the ~6% margin in the odds. Costs: provincial levy (~6–9% of GGR), 15% VAT, income tax, platform/data/payment fees, and a ~0.1% responsible-gambling contribution. (See `docs/operator-payouts.md`.)
