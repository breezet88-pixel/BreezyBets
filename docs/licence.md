# Getting the bookmaker licence

_Researched 16 November 2026 from public sources. Most detail comes from licensing guides and law-firm notes, not from the regulators themselves, so every figure here needs confirming with the board or a gambling lawyer. This is not legal advice._

## What the licence is

- South Africa has no national betting licence. Each province's gambling board licenses bookmakers.
- A bookmaker licence covers **fixed-odds bets on sport and racing**. It does not cover casino-style games online, which remain illegal ([National Gambling Board](https://www.ngb.org.za/media-releases/interactive-gambling-is-illegal-in-south-africa/)). Our six games stay demo-only.
- Licensing guides say a licence from one province is used to take online bets from players country-wide. The October 2025 Supreme Court of Appeal ruling shows that what a licence allows depends on that province's own Act ([Werksmans](https://werksmans.com/supreme-court-of-appeal-clarifies-boundaries-between-casino-and-bookmaker-licences-in-the-gauteng-province/)), so have the lawyer confirm the reach of whichever licence you apply for.

## The routes

| Route | What I found | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Western Cape, new licence** | The board accepts bookmaker applications at any time and does not require a betting shop for an online site ([Chambers](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/gaming-law-2025/south-africa)). The provincial minister confirmed in October 2025 that bookmakers may apply directly ([Business Report](https://businessreport.co.za/news/2025-10-23-the-dark-side-of-the-western-capes-r402bn-gambling-boom--and-whats-driving-it/)). | **Recommended first choice** |
| Mpumalanga | Invited proposals in April 2025 for 20 new licences, with offices required in the province ([Werksmans](https://werksmans.com/sports-betting-opportunity-mpumalanga-economic-regulator-invites-proposals-for-bookmaker-licences/)). I could not find the closing date or the outcome. | Ask whether the round is still open |
| Eastern Cape | Trade press describes on-demand licensing since 2025 ([SiGMA](https://sigma.world/news/topbet-enters-south-africas-eastern-cape-market/)); a consultancy says applications are only taken in formal rounds ([source](https://www.zitadelleag.com/services/igaming/south-africa-bookmaker)). | Conflicting: ask the board |
| Buy or join an existing licensed bookmaker | Taking over a licensed business or a controlling share needs the board's approval ([National Gambling Act, section 59](https://api.acts.co.za/national-gambling-act-2004/59__licence_transfers.php)). Running a brand under someone else's licence may still need your own licence, depending on who actually runs the betting. | Possible shortcut; needs a lawyer |

## Western Cape: what it involves

**Fees** reported by licensing guides, from Provincial Notice 113 of 2026 ([guide 1](https://gamblingmaps.org/map/regulations/south-africa/for-operators/how-to-get-a-license), [guide 2](https://vantegris.com/services/south-africa/licenses/gambling-licenses/)):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Application fee | R16,343 |
| Refundable deposit | R31,982 |
| Licence fee, each year | R3,278 |
| Investigation fee, each year | R13,088 |

The board published a draft change to its fees in October 2026 ([draft](https://www.wcgrb.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Draft-Amendment-FEES-AND-COSTS-2026.pdf)), so these may be about to change. They are the small part of the cost: lawyers, the certified betting platform and working capital are larger, and I found no reliable figures for those.

**What the board looks at**
- **The company.** A licence goes only to a registered company or close corporation. Guides add a South African bank account and an office in the province. Banks treat gambling businesses as high risk, so open the account early.
- **The people.** A background ("probity") investigation covers the company, anyone holding 5% or more, and key staff, who need their own licences ([board forms](https://wcgrb.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Application%20Forms/Racing%20and%20Betting/Business%20Entity%20Disclosure%20(BED)%20form%20(New%20application).pdf)). Expect questions on criminal record, tax compliance, solvency and where the money comes from.
- **The paperwork.** Business entity disclosure form, founding documents, a diagram of ownership up to the real owners, a business plan, proof of funding, and policies for FICA, responsible gambling and POPIA.
- **Premises.** A separate premises application, with a signed lease and the landlord's consent.
- **The system.** The betting platform must be certified (see `docs/competitive-analysis.md`, section 6).
- **Time.** One guide estimates 6 to 12 months, mostly for the investigation. A 2023 review said demand was straining the board.

## Risks to know before spending money

1. **A freeze has been called for.** In March 2026 an MP asked for a moratorium on new bookmaker licences after 3,135 gambling licences were issued in a year, 549 of them in the Western Cape ([TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/2026-03-22-more-than-3000-gambling-licences-issued-annually-as-rise-mzansi-raises-oversight-concern/)). I found no sign it was adopted, and no statement from the board. **Confirm the board is still accepting applications before paying anyone.**
2. **A possible 20% national tax** on online betting revenue, to be decided in the February 2027 Budget.
3. **The law is moving.** A national remote-gambling framework is expected, and it could change who licenses online betting.

## The order to do it in

1. **Email the board** (draft below). Free, and it answers the biggest unknown.
2. **Get quotes from two gambling lawyers.** Names that came up: [Lurie Inc](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/gaming-law-2025/south-africa), a specialist gambling practice in Johannesburg, and Werksmans, which publishes on bookmaker licensing. I have not verified either firm's current services or fees.
3. **Decide the ownership** with the lawyer, including black-ownership (B-BBEE) expectations.
4. **Register the company, open the bank account, find the office.**
5. **Choose the certified platform** and get its certification letter.
6. **Submit** the application, the premises application and the key-employee applications, and pay the fees.
7. **Investigation and decision.** Only then connect payments and switch off demo mode.

## Email to the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board

Send to the licensing contact listed on wcgrb.co.za.

> Subject: Enquiry: new online bookmaker licence application
>
> Good day
>
> We intend to apply for a bookmaker licence to operate an online sportsbook (sport and racing only, no casino-style games). Before preparing the application, could you please confirm:
>
> 1. Is the Board currently accepting new bookmaker licence applications?
> 2. Which forms are required for a new online-only bookmaker, and which fees and deposits currently apply?
> 3. Is an office or licensed premises in the Western Cape required for an online-only operation, and what must it consist of?
> 4. Which shareholders, directors and staff need individual probity clearance or key-employee licences?
> 5. What is the present processing time from submission to decision?
> 6. Are there ownership or empowerment requirements we should plan for?
> 7. What certification must the betting system hold before the licence can be used?
>
> Thank you.
